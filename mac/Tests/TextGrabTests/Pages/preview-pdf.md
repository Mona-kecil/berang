# Life in the Water

Otters are distinguished by their long, slim bodies, powerful webbed feet for swimming, and their dense fur, which keeps them warm and buoyant.

Some species are adapted to cold waters and need a high metabolic rate to stay warm. They eat fish, frogs, birds, and shellfish, depending on where they live and what the season brings. Young otters stay with their family for about a year. They can live up to sixteen years in the wild.

# How Otters Hunt

Otters find prey with their whiskers, which sense movement in murky water. Sea otters use stones as tools to open shells.

People hunted otters for their pelts for centuries, and some species are still endangered today.
